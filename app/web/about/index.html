Gumption: Task Starter

Can't get started? Gumption picks one task, shrinks it to a first step and times it, so starting is the easy part.

Open Gumption in your browser
Also for Android, iPhone, iPad, Mac and Windows. No account needed.

What it does

  • Picks one task for you from the time you have, whether you can leave the house and your energy, and says why.
  • Shrinks it to a two-minute first step, with ideas for common tasks like calls, forms and errands.
  • Times it with a visual timer. Commit to just five minutes if that's all you've got.
  • Rewards starting with XP and rewards you choose. Streaks never reset to zero.
  • Keeps Personal and Work apart, each syncing to its own Google account if you want.
  • Captures in seconds: type "call the bank tomorrow 2pm 15 min", speak it, or brain-dump a whole list.

How it uses Google

Signing in with Google is optional and only turns on sync. Gumption then stores your tasks in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, so your devices stay in step. It can't see your other Drive files, and there's no Gumption server: the developer never receives your tasks. Details are in the privacy policy.
