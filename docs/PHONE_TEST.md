# 15-minute phone test (Android)

A quick pass over the features that have only been checked in tests and web screenshots. Run it after installing a new build. Use made-up tasks only (no patient details). Do it in the regular (personal) copy of the app; the work-profile copy only needs step 1.

To report: reply with the step numbers that failed and a screenshot of each. "All pass" is a fine answer too.

**Before you start:** install the latest build from `…/android-latest/Gumption.apk` over the current app.

## 1. After the update (1 min)
1.1 The icon is a **gold four-point star on night blue**, and the name under it says **Gumption**. If your phone uses themed icons (Android 13+, Wallpaper & style → Themed icons), the star takes the theme colour.
1.2 The app opens on the night-sky look (or the cream **Dawn** look if your phone is in light mode).
1.3 Progress → the gear (top right) → Settings → Sync: Personal still shows as connected, with no "Reconnect". (Give it up to a minute; it retries by itself.) In the work-profile copy, Work is still connected.
1.4 Your tasks are still there.

## 2. Start the timer first, so it runs while you test (1 min)
2.1 Tap the gold **+** → type `Test timer` → **Add & start**.
2.2 A star lights up with **"A new star."**, and after about 2 seconds the focus timer appears (tapping skips the wait).
2.3 On the focus screen, tap the hand-and-heart icon at the top (**Hold me to it**) → **Share**. The share sheet opens with "I'm starting "Test timer" now, for 15 min…". Back out without sending.
2.4 Leave the focus screen running and press the home button. Carry on with step 3. **Step 9 checks the alert.**

## 3. Today (1 min)
3.1 Today shows one task: its name in big type, the step you're on, a big **Start**, **5 min**, and under them **Not this one · Smaller · Later**.
3.2 Tap the button at the top left (e.g. `30 min · Staying in`): a **Right now** sheet opens for time, staying in or going out, energy and which tasks. Change the time and tap **Pick for me**; the task re-picks.
3.3 **Not this one** shows a different task. Tap it once more: Gumption asks you to pick one of three.
3.4 **Smaller**: a sheet lets you rewrite the step smaller. **Later**: the task goes away and a message says it comes back in 3 hours.

## 4. Quick add (2 min)
4.1 Tap +. You see the task box, Personal/Work side by side, **How long?** and **More options**. No Priority yet.
4.2 Type `Call the bank tomorrow 2pm 15 min urgent`. Chips appear for tomorrow 2pm, 15m and Critical.
4.3 Tap **More options**: Priority, dread and **Need to leave the house** appear. Close it, reopen Quick add: it remembers.
4.4 Tap the mic and say "buy printer paper". It types it. (Allow the microphone if asked.)
4.5 Add it. It appears in Tasks.

## 5. Tasks (2 min)
5.1 At the top right there are two square buttons: lightning (Brain dump) and the filter. No rows of pills.
5.2 Tap the filter → choose **Work** → **Done**. Only Work tasks show, and a pill under the title says **Work**. Set it back to Everything.
5.3 In the same sheet, choose **Board**, then **Done**: the board shows. Switch back to **List**.
5.4 Each row has a round-cornered check box, the title, and a short line (coloured dot, due date in gold, length).
5.5 Swipe a test task right: it's marked done, with **Undo** at the bottom. Tap Undo.
5.6 Swipe a test task left: it's deleted, with **Undo**. Tap Undo.

## 6. A task's editor (1 min)
6.1 Open a task with no first step. Under **First 2-minute step** there are idea chips (e.g. for "Call the bank": *+ Find the number and put it on screen*). Tap one; it fills in.
6.2 Under **Steps**, tap **Suggest steps**: a few steps appear, and the page doesn't jump. Tap **Save**.
6.3 Under **Details**, tap **Priority**: a small sheet opens. Pick one and tap **Done**.

## 7. Share into the app (1 min)
7.1 In Chrome (or Messages), select some text → **Share** → **Gumption**.
7.2 Gumption opens with Quick add prefilled. There's only one copy of the app open (the Recents screen shows one Gumption).

## 8. Home-screen widget (2 min)
8.1 Widgets you placed before this update now show the new look (dark navy, or cream in light mode).
8.2 Long-press the home screen → Widgets → **Gumption**. Four sizes. Add the **4×2** one.
8.3 It shows the same task as Today: workspace (and "Step x of y" if it has a checklist), the title, up to two steps, **Start** and **5 min**, and on the right **Add a task** and your streak.
8.4 Tap **Start**: the focus timer opens on that task. Tap **5 min**: a 5-minute session. Tap **Add a task**: Quick add opens.
8.5 Switch the phone to light mode (or dark): the widget follows.

## 9. Timer alert (check when it's due)
9.1 About 15 minutes after step 2, a "time's up" notification arrives even with the app in the background or the screen off.
9.2 Tapping it opens the app.
If it's late or missing: Android Settings → Apps → Gumption → Battery → **Unrestricted**, then try a 5-minute timer (**5 min** on Today).

## 10. Evening and errands (2 min)
10.1 After 5 pm, Today shows **Wrap up** at the bottom. Tap it: stars for what you started today, then pick tomorrow's first task and tap **Done for today**. Tomorrow morning, Today should offer that task first.
10.2 In the editor, turn on **Leave the house** for two test tasks and give each a place (Pharmacy, Post office). On Today, set Right now to **Can go out** and 1h. An **Errand run** line appears; tap it to see both stops.

## 11. Backup and guide (1 min)
11.1 Settings → Backup → **Save a backup file** → save it to Downloads. A confirmation appears.
11.2 **Restore from a backup file** → pick that file → it says how many tasks it has → **Restore** (leave "Also restore its settings" off). Nothing changes: a restore only adds back what's missing, and it's the same data.
11.3 Settings → Help → **How Gumption works**: it opens, and a **Go to…** button jumps to that screen.
11.4 Settings → **Focus timer** → **Blocks**. Start a test task: the timer is a row of blocks, one per minute. Set it back to **Orbit** if you prefer.

## 12. Links, Related and your sky (2 min)
12.1 Notes → the note button at the top → in the note, type `Call about [[` and a few letters of a test task's name. A bar above the keyboard offers it. Tap it: the link turns gold.
12.2 Under the note, **Links** shows the task. Tap it: the task opens, and its **Related** section lists your note.
12.3 Type `[[Buy stamps]]` (no such task). Its row says "Not a task or note yet"; tap **Make task**. It now appears in Tasks.
12.4 Progress → **Your sky**: a star for each task you've started. Start a test task and come back: a new star.

## Clean up
Delete the test tasks (swipe left) and remove the widget if you don't want it.
